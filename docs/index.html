Proud Ground Grant & Funding Tracker

A living list of grant and funding opportunities matched to Proud Ground’s mission—affordable homeownership for first-time and BIPOC buyers across Oregon and SW Washington. Each opportunity links to where to apply, what’s needed, and a downloadable summary.

📅 Download calendar (.ics) ➕ Add to Google Calendar
How to use the calendar: Download the .ics file above, then in Google Calendar go to Settings → Import & Export → Import and select the file. Every grant deadline (with a 30-day reminder) drops into your calendar. Deadlines marked “verify” are recurring programs whose next exact date should be confirmed on the funder’s site.

Disclaimer: This tracker is a research aid, not legal or financial advice. Deadlines, amounts, and eligibility change frequently—always confirm details on the funder’s official page before applying. Several entries are recurring programs whose next cycle dates are estimated from the most recent published round.

Built for Proud Ground (EIN 93-1290320), a 501(c)(3) Community Land Trust. Data compiled June 26, 2026. Edit grants-data.json to update; the site, stats, and calendar all read from it.
